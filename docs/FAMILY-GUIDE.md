# Family Member Guide — How to Use the Family Archive

This guide is written for regular family members. It covers the everyday things you'll do: exploring the family tree, looking at photos, and — most importantly — **sharing your own memories and corrections**.

> **Good to know:** you can browse everything without an account. You only need to sign in when you want to *add* something — a photo, a correction, a new family member.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the app (on your computer, or from your phone on the same Wi-Fi).
2. Tap **Sign in** (top-right on a phone, bottom-left of the sidebar on a computer).
3. Enter the email and password you were given (or that you registered with).
4. Tick **Remember me** if it's your own device.

**Don't have an account yet?** Tap **Request access**, fill in your name and email, and an administrator will approve you. You'll be able to sign in once approved.

**Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email link.

---

## 2. Finding your way around

The important sections, and where they live:

| Section | What it's for | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| **Family Tree** | See the whole family across generations | Sidebar (computer) or bottom bar on a phone |
| **Family** | Search for any family member | Sidebar / bottom bar |
| **Photos** | Browse and upload family photos | Sidebar / bottom bar |
| **Reunions** | Reunion events and photo albums | Sidebar / bottom bar |
| **My Profile** | Your own page, change your photo | Computer: sidebar · Phone: **More** |
| **Contributions** | Track things you've submitted | Computer: sidebar · Phone: **More** |
| **Notifications** | See when your submissions get approved | The 🔔 bell in the header |

On a **phone**, the four main sections are the bottom bar: **Tree · Family · Photos · Reunions**. Everything else lives under **More** (the button at the far right).

---

## 3. Exploring the family tree

This is the heart of the app — the whole family drawn across generations (parents above children, spouses side by side).

- **Move around:** drag the tree, pinch to zoom on a phone, or use the **+ / −** buttons.
- **Find someone:** use the **search box** at the top of the tree and type a name. Click a result and the tree jumps to that person.
- **See someone's details:** tap any person's card — a panel opens with their parents, spouse, children, and a link to their full profile.
- **Tidy up a big branch:** use the **+ / −** on a card to collapse or expand that person's descendants.
- **Focus view (great on a phone):** tap a person and the tree zooms to just them and their closest family, with buttons to show/hide **Parents · Siblings · Spouse · Children**. Tap **Full Tree** to go back to everyone.

---

## 4. Looking up family members

- The **Family** page lists everyone, with a search box — search by name, nickname, or family branch.
- Each person's **profile page** shows their photo, birth/death years, biography, and their family connections (parents, children, spouse, siblings, grandparents).
- Sections can be **expanded and collapsed** — tap a heading like *Biography* or *Children* to open or close it.
- **Note on privacy:** for living family members, some details (exact birth date, location) are only shown to administrators. This is deliberate.

---

## 5. Photos — browsing and sharing

### Browsing
- Open **Photos** to see the whole archive — tap any photo to view it full-screen.
- In the viewer: **swipe left/right** for the next/previous photo, **pinch or double-tap** to zoom in.
- Below the photo you'll see the caption, date, location, and any **tagged family members** — tap a name to open that person's profile.
- You can **favorite** photos you love (the heart icon) and **download** them if the administrator has allowed it.

### Uploading your own photos
1. Sign in and tap **Upload Photos** (or drag & drop on a computer).
2. On a phone you'll see **Take Photo** (uses your camera) and **Choose Photos** (your photo library — you can pick several at once).
3. Preview your selection; remove any you don't want.
4. Add a caption, date, and location. You can also **tag family members** who appear in the photo.
5. Submit.

> If the administrator has photo approval turned on, your photo will show as **pending** until someone approves it. You can watch its status in **Contributions → Pending**.

---

## 6. Reunions — events and albums

- The **Reunions** page lists past and upcoming family gatherings.
- Open an event to see its date, location, description, and **albums** (like *Group Photos* or *Family Dinner*).
- Tap an album to browse its gallery.
- **Add your own reunion photos:** sign in, tap **Upload Photos** on the event page, choose which album they belong in, add captions/tags, and submit. (They'll be pending approval if that's enabled.)

---

## 7. Suggesting corrections and adding family

If you know something in the family records is wrong — or a family member is missing — you can **suggest** the fix. You can't change the official tree yourself, and that's by design: it keeps the records accurate.

1. Open the person's profile and tap **Suggest a correction** (or use it from anywhere to add a new member).
2. Describe the change — e.g. *"Pedro Cruz is actually the son of Juan Cruz"* — and add a short reason or where you learned it.
3. Submit. It enters the **Pending** queue, and an administrator reviews it (they may ask for more information).

Nothing you suggest changes the official tree until an administrator approves it — your submission is just a proposal.

---

## 8. Tracking your contributions

The **Contributions** page lists everything you've submitted, with three tabs:

- **Pending** — waiting for an administrator to review.
- **Approved** — accepted and now part of the official archive.
- **Rejected** — not accepted (the reviewer's note, if any, is shown).

This is also where you'll see the status of your uploaded photos.

---

## 9. Notifications

The 🔔 bell in the header shows a red badge when something new arrives. You'll be notified when:

- One of your contributions or photos is **approved or rejected**
- Someone **tags you** in a photo
- A **new reunion** is announced

---

## 10. Quick tips

- **Use it on your phone like an app:** in your browser menu, choose **Add to Home Screen** — it installs like an app.
- **On a phone,** always use the bottom bar to move around — don't scroll looking for menus.
- **Uploading from a phone?** The **Take Photo** button uses your camera directly — great for snapping reunion moments on the spot.
- **Made a typo in a suggestion?** Don't worry — an administrator will review it before anything changes, and rejected items don't affect the records.
- **Privacy:** only approved family members can see the archive's photos and personal details, and location data in photos is automatically removed on upload.

---

*If something isn't working or you're not sure what you're allowed to do, ask your family's administrator — they manage accounts, approvals, and settings.*
